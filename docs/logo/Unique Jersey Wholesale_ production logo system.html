Unique Jersey Wholesale logo system

The circled UJ with a diagonal blade, rebuilt on the 64-unit grid, the Inter wordmark and the navy, white and cyan palette of the ERP.

Three refinements of the circled concept

All three keep the UJ and the diagonal blade. Variation 1 is the one carried forward.

Variation 1: closest to the circled concept

Heavy italic U and J with one blade crossing both. The blade is symmetric with two sharp tips, so it reads as a slash, not a check-mark.

Variation 2: modern and minimal

Lighter stems, thinner blade, less slant. The quietest fit for dense ERP screens.

Variation 3: aggressive and athletic

Steeper slant, heaviest stems, a thicker blade and a second speed line.

Final brand system

Variation 1 with slightly heavier stems, so the counters stay open at small sizes. The J stem rises above the U, as in the logo-system document.

Usage

  • Clear space around the mark: one stem width.
  • Below 24 px tall, use the icon on its own; below 48 px, use the favicon tile.
  • UNIQUE in cool white, JERSEY in cyan, WHOLESALE in muted blue-gray. On light backgrounds the cyan becomes #0891B2.
  • The blade is cut out of the letters with a small gap, so it works on any background and in one colour.
